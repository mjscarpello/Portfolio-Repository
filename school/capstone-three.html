Bellevue University · DSC 450 · Project 03

NYC dry recycling across communities.

How do community characteristics relate to dry recycling share across New York City, and what could help predict that share?

Role Data Wrangler · Group 1Status In progressCollection School

THE QUESTION

Making community data comparable.

Our team is exploring the relationship between neighborhood characteristics and dry recycling share. My responsibility is preparing consistent, documented collection and neighborhood data that can support modeling and visualization.

I'm working with NYC Department of Sanitation monthly collection records and supporting neighborhood indicators, checking definitions, time periods, geographic units, and missing values.

WRANGLING

What I am preparing

Validated collection records, a documented target definition, matched community indicators, and data-quality notes for the team.

LIMITATION

Geographic alignment

DSNY community districts and socioeconomic sub-borough geographies do not match one-to-one. Any crosswalk requires assumptions and validation.

PROJECT IN PROGRESS

Evidence and results.

Final model comparisons and figures will be added after team validation. No preliminary finding is presented as final.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

Collection-based dry recycling share is not the same as a capture rate or total waste diversion rate. Other waste streams and mismatched geographic boundaries can change comparisons. This is ongoing team research, not a completed predictive study.

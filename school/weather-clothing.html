Weather-Based Delivery Clothing Predictor.

A classification prototype explored how weather and delivery conditions could inform clothing choices for a driver before a shift.

RolePredictive modeling & applicationStatusCourse project · prototypeCollectionSchool

THE QUESTION

Why this project?

Could a model suggest practical delivery clothing from the weather and expected work conditions?

I explored a categorical predictor using weather and route-related features, with feature engineering and model evaluation for Bellevue University’s DSC 355 Predictive Analytics class.

INSIGHT

What the work shows

The project demonstrates a complete machine-learning thought process and the possibility of an interactive clothing recommendation interface.

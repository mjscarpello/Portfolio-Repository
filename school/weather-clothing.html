Bellevue University · DSC 355 · Predictive Analytics

Weather-Based Delivery Clothing Predictor.

I deployed a tuned Random Forest in Streamlit to recommend a driver shirt category from route and weather conditions, with a transparent explanation of the prediction.

RolePredictive modeling & applicationStatus Streamlit prototype · Milestone 5CollectionSchool

THE QUESTION

Why this project?

Could a driver choose clothing more consistently by accounting for weather and route conditions before leaving?

I engineered 34 model inputs, trained a multiclass Random Forest, and deployed the saved pipeline in Streamlit with weather lookup, manual scenarios, and probability explanations.

INSIGHT

What the work shows

Held-out accuracy was 0.682, balanced accuracy 0.686, and macro F1 0.662. The deployment checks the feature schema and excludes leakage-prone clothing fields.

CONTRIBUTION

My role

Independent data preparation, feature engineering, model evaluation, and Streamlit app development.

EVIDENCE, NOT DECORATION

Figures & work samples.

Original charts and screenshots. Select any image to enlarge it.

WHAT TO KEEP IN MIND

The modeling data is synthetic; reported test metrics measure an internal holdout, not verified performance on real-world driver decisions.

01 / THE PURPOSE

Make complex material easier to understand.

Educational media has to do more than look appealing. It has to help someone recognize a concept, follow an explanation, or practice what they have learned.

Working on health-science learning materials introduced me to the challenge of communicating precise information in an accessible format. The examples here show different approaches: animated explanations, visual demonstrations, and interactive terminology practice.

06 / REFLECTION

What the experience taught me.

Visual communication is a practical skill. An instructional animation or interface is only useful when people can follow the idea it is meant to teach.

Technology and education shape each other. Interactive media asks you to think about both how a system responds and what a person learns from that response.

Feedback is part of the work. Collaborating on educational materials means staying open to revisions that make the result clearer for the intended audience.

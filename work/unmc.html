06 / LEARNING FROM FEEDBACK

See the experience through someone else's eyes.

During accessibility reviews, my colleagues helped me recognize issues that could otherwise go unnoticed when designing for my own way of using technology.

01 / Perception

Readable information

Consider text size, visual clarity and whether meaningful text is available to a screen reader.

02 / Navigation

Usable interactions

Consider where buttons are placed, how scrolling behaves and how the learner moves through a module.

03 / Context

Different learners and devices

Consider how people with different abilities and devices may experience the same educational content.

The lesson: accessibility is part of communicating well, not an afterthought. Feedback helped me recognize perspectives I had not initially considered.

08 / REFLECTION

What I carry into data science.

Consider the audience. People interpret information differently. I aim to make visualizations clear, concise and accessible without leaving unnecessary room for assumptions.

Consider the implications. The work taught me to look beyond my own perspective and think about the ethical implications of decisions and how different groups may experience their outcomes.

Build trust through the work. Staying organized, communicating progress and listening to feedback are foundations of successful collaboration. These principles continue to shape my approach to data science.

THE IDEA

Learning by doing.

I wanted medical terminology practice to feel like an interactive challenge rather than a list of definitions. Players respond to questions and work through game-like scenarios where choices matter.

The project connected healthcare education, visual design, gameplay feedback, and my early understanding of program structure.

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

The question comes first.

I am drawn to projects that connect careful analysis with real situations: storm damage, electricity forecasts, package delivery, and learning technology. I enjoy understanding what a result means and finding the clearest way to show it.

My work experience reminds me that every dataset describes people, processes, and choices. I want to combine scientific thinking, practical experience, and creativity to make systems more useful to the communities they serve.

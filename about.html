Science, data,
and curiosity.

I am a neuroscientist-turned data scientist. I'm captivated by modern technology, and I want to build systems that make a practical difference for the people who use them.

HOW I GOT HERE

The questions have always mattered to me.

My background in neuroscience helped shape the way I approach a problem. I enjoy asking questions, examining evidence, and understanding how complex systems work. Data science gave me another way to explore those interests through programming, statistics, predictive modeling, and visualization.

I am drawn to work that brings technical analysis back to real decisions. I think the most meaningful projects combine curiosity with the discipline to test assumptions, explain what the results actually mean, and acknowledge what remains uncertain.

My longer-term goal is to use data science to improve civic life and empower communities. That includes making information easier to understand and building tools that help people make better decisions.
